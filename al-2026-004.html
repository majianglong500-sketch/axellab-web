第九师校园文化建设Campus Culture, Ninth Division

让校训被看见。161 团、164 团校园户外标识、文化墙与室内浮雕的设计与落地记录。Making the school motto visible. Outdoor signage, culture walls and indoor reliefs for the 161st and 164th Regiments.

01 设计目标Design Goal

将校风校训转化为校园中可识别的空间元素。户外以远距离识别为主，室内侧重近距离的材质与细节。Turn the school motto into legible spatial elements: distance legibility outdoors, material detail indoors.

02 建模Modeling

在 Rhino 中确定立体字体量、弧线造型与浮雕层次，并按现场尺寸和安装节点拆分构件。Letter volume, arc forms and relief layers were set in Rhino, then split by site dimensions and fixing points.

03 加工Fabrication

文字构件、浮雕板与格栅在车间完成雕刻和边框处理，表面保留适度的加工纹理。Text panels, relief boards and grids were carved and framed in the workshop, keeping a light tool texture.

04 落地Installation

完成 164 团广场立体字、161 团校风校训墙与文化展示墙，以及室内浮雕、艺术走廊和格栅安装。Plaza lettering, motto and culture walls outdoors; reliefs, art corridor and grids indoors.
