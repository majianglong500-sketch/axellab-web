关于About

不只是作品集，而是一个持续生长的实验室。Not only a portfolio. A growing laboratory.

AXEL LAB 由马江龙创立并运营，常驻中国新疆乌鲁木齐。AXEL LAB is a computational design practice by Ma Jianglong, based in Urumqi, Xinjiang, China.

这里的工作连接 Rhino、Grasshopper、Python、AI 工作流与数字制造。每个项目都尽量完整记录：设计目标、参数化逻辑、自动化脚本、视觉探索、加工限制和最终成果。The work connects Rhino, Grasshopper, Python, AI workflows and digital fabrication. Each project is documented as a full process: design goal, parametric logic, automation, visual exploration, fabrication constraints and final outcome.

AXEL LAB 是一个持续生长的实验室，用来测试计算如何从屏幕走向材料，从模型走向真实建造。AXEL LAB is a growing laboratory for testing how computation can move from screen to material, from model to built result.
