医药企业形象空间Pharmaceutical Brand Space

常春泰医药门头、大厅与公共区域的品牌形象设计与安装。Storefront, lobby and public-area brand graphics for Changchuntai Pharma.

门头形象墙灯光现场安装

01 项目概况01 Overview

项目涵盖门头、大厅形象墙、文化墙及楼梯灯光。各区域沿用统一的品牌色与字体。Covers storefront, lobby identity wall, culture wall and stair lighting, using one brand palette and typeface.

02 空间与灯光02 Space & Lighting

门头兼顾日间识别与夜间发光效果；室内以灯光强调形象墙与楼梯转角。The storefront works by day and at night; indoor lighting highlights the identity wall and stair corners.

03 方案与落地03 Design & Delivery

会议区战略墙与背景墙目前为方案效果图，其余为现场完工照片。Meeting-area walls are shown as renders; other images are site photos.
